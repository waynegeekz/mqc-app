# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Property developers and owners commissioning construction or building projects. They are shortlisting a project management firm to take a project from feasibility to handover, and the site's job is to get them to request a consultation.

## Product Purpose

MQC Project Management plans, procures and controls construction and building projects from feasibility to handover. The marketing site exists to win consultation requests. Success is a qualified enquiry submitted through the contact form.

## Positioning

Cost and schedule rigour. MQC reports progress against a baseline and stands behind "Built to schedule. Delivered to budget." One team carries a project through all six phases, so nothing is lost between handoffs.

## Operating Context

Clients are weighing a significant capital commitment and want to see that risk is controlled. Work is organised in six phases: Feasibility, Design, Procurement, Construction, Commissioning, Handover. Typical artefacts are tender packages, baselines, defect lists, as-built records, manuals, warranties and final accounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Services, About, Contact (Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, tokens in `src/app/tokens.css`).
- Contact page runs an enquiry form with name, email, organisation and message. Reply is by email.
- Copy source of truth is `src/lib/site.ts` (name, tagline, promise, nav, CTA, phases).
- Primary CTA label: "Request a consultation".
- About page copy is currently a placeholder and needs real content from the client.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MQC Project Management (short: MQC).
- Tagline: "Every phase accounted for." Promise: "Built to schedule. Delivered to budget."
- Voice in existing copy is plain and direct, with risks reported "plainly".
- Existing asset: `public/brand/logo-reversed.svg` (reversed logo, for dark backgrounds).

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no case studies, client names, testimonials, credentials, figures or team details. Future work must not fabricate any of these; use structure and process (the six phases) as the proof instead, and leave clearly marked gaps for real content.

## Product Principles

- Rigour is shown, not claimed: specifics about how cost, schedule and quality are controlled beat adjectives.
- Every page moves toward one action, requesting a consultation.
- Never invent proof. Absent evidence stays absent until the client supplies it.
- Plain, direct language that a developer can act on.
